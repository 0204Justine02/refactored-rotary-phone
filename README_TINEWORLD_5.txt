TineWorld 5.0 Sandbox Upgrade

This version adds a large offline sandbox layer on top of the existing TineHub project.

Major systems:
- Seeded procedural terrain with forest, desert, snow, jungle, plains, beach, rivers, mountains and caves.
- Chunk-style / virtual infinite terrain generation outside the initial cached area.
- Villages, named NPCs, simple schedules, merchant trading and quests.
- Animals with wandering and reproduction; enemies with night spawning and chasing.
- Hunger, stamina, health regeneration, temperature, weather, day/night and fall damage.
- Cooking and food.
- 36-slot inventory, stacking, hotbar, drag/swap inventory, equipment slots and armor.
- Building blocks: planks, stone, glass, doors, windows, chests, chairs, stairs, slabs and torches.
- Rotation, build preview, copy/paste, persistent building saves.
- Sword, axe, spear, bow and shield combat; critical hits, blocking, knockback, HP bars and loot.
- Dungeon, boss arena, ruins, hidden treasure, coordinates, map, player marker and waypoints.
- Boat, horse, minecart and car movement modes plus fast travel.
- Three local world save slots, new/rename/delete/continue, seed persistence and auto-save.
- Multiplayer-ready data fields for player names/HP/chat/trading architecture; no network multiplayer is enabled.
- Animated walk, jump, attack, mine, build, eat and ranged-attack states.
- Landscape-friendly responsive UI and existing TineHub games retained.

Controls:
WASD / Arrow keys = move
Space = jump
F = attack
Q = mine
B = build
R = bow
E = interact
Shift = shield/block
Mobile controls are available in the TineWorld page.

All gameplay is offline. Progress is stored in localStorage on the device.
